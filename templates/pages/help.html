Glossary

Dispatch Terms Explained

A dispatcher is a professional who finds loads, negotiates rates, handles paperwork, and coordinates logistics so carriers can focus on driving.

A rate confirmation is a legal agreement between the carrier and broker/shipper that confirms the agreed-upon rate and terms for a specific load.

Deadhead refers to miles driven without a paying load. Our route planning minimizes deadhead to maximize your earnings.

A factoring company pays carriers immediately (minus a fee) for their invoices, so you don't have to wait 30-60 days for broker payment.

Professional Driving Permit is a South African requirement for drivers of vehicles used for reward (commercial) transport.
